§ 01 — About

A non-linear journey, still in motion

Who · Why

I've always been fascinated by technology and its power to solve real problems. That curiosity led me to co-found Invoid — a venture that caught the eye of Y Combinator (W21) and went on to a successful acquisition.

Since then I've operated at senior product roles inside a regulated lender and a private-markets platform — but the deeper pull has always been the capital side of the table. Today I'm SEBI-certified (NISM XIX-D, AIF Cat I & II) and spend most of my time on private investments, fund work, and advising founders.

I back early-stage AI, fintech, and consumer businesses where I have real conviction — and pass quietly where I don't. Always up for a good conversation about the next big idea, or the next quiet, contrarian one.
